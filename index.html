Terry Cheuk‑Fung Yip, PhD

Co‑Director (Data Science), MDAC; Associate Professor

Medical Data Analytics Centre (MDAC); Department of Medicine & Therapeutics, The Chinese University of Hong Kong.

Clinical researcher focusing on chronic hepatitis B (CHB) and metabolic dysfunction–associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD), leveraging large‑scale real‑world healthcare data and advanced analytics for risk assessment and outcome prediction. He has helped establish one of the largest long‑term CHB cohorts with HBsAg seroclearance and serves as a Principal Investigator of the international VCTE‑Prognosis study on non‑invasive tests for MASLD outcomes. Awards include APASL Young Investigator (2018, 2022, 2023), AASLD Early Career Investigator (2020), and EASL Best Poster – Viral Hepatitis (2024).
